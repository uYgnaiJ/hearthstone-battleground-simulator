# Battlegrounds presentation references

Reviewed during the native presentation work:

- [Blizzard 22.4 patch notes](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23761712): official animated board and finisher previews. The visible opposing hero anchors are centered at the top and bottom; health sits on the lower right of each portrait. The cosmetic finisher is reference material, not the animation reproduced here.
- [Official Jade Gardens board preview](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/tl/TL5NUC9O2G831643933472876.gif): framed physical board with separate corner decorations and hero recesses.
- [Official finisher preview](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/content_entry_media/o2/O2WZT99C6OY11643919222326.gif): hero placement and focus of end-of-combat effects.
- [PC Gamer's original Battlegrounds guide](https://www.pcgamer.com/how-to-play-hearthstones-battlegrounds-mode/): surviving minion tiers add to hero attack before the hero strikes.

Local implementation uses an original board and procedural effects. The four-panel roll is an approximation of the phase transition, not an extracted original animation. Aura light is composed behind battlefield portraits. Enlarged cards and hand cards do not receive this light.
